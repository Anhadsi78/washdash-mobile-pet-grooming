Mobile pet grooming in the GTA: booking a driveway groom on WashDash

WashDash is a Greater Toronto Area marketplace for mobile car detailing and mobile pet grooming. The groomer comes to your driveway, so the dog does not need a crate or a car ride to a salon.

What you can book

Grooming prices are set by the groomer, not by WashDash, and each groomer's full menu shows on their calendar before you pick a slot. Two common visits on the homepage are:

  • Full groom: bath, haircut, nails and ears.
  • Bath and brush: wash, blow-dry and brush-out, for the visits between full grooms.

The site also flags de-shedding, nail care and anxious-dog-friendly visits among the reasons people book at home.

How a booking works

Enter your postal code to see vetted, insured pros who cover your street, with their live calendars. If a slot is visible, it is actually free; confirmation is instant. You do not need an account: you book with your name, contact details and address, then manage, reschedule or cancel from the link in your confirmation. Your card is charged when the job is done, and the pro keeps the payment.

Booking is free for customers, and WashDash does not take a commission from providers. The marketplace is seeding the GTA first; you can request service elsewhere in Canada, and the site will try to match you if there is no open calendar yet. Start on the WashDash homepage and choose the dog side to see grooms near you.
